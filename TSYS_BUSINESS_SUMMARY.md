# T'sys Industrial Controls Inc. — Business & Product Summary

## Company Overview

**T'sys Industrial Controls Inc.** is a Philippine industrial B2B supplier and electrical equipment fabricator established in **2002**. It is a corporate affiliate of **Total Power Box Solution Inc.** and operates as one of the country's leading fabrication and manufacturing companies for electrical and power control systems. The company categorizes itself as a **"Commercial & Industrial Equipment Supplier"** on its official Facebook page (facebook.com/tsysindustrial).

The company serves commercial and industrial clients, providing end-to-end capability from product supply to custom engineering, design, and installation.

## What the Business Does

T'sys operates in two complementary areas:

### 1. Product Distribution
T'sys is the authorized Philippine distributor/supplier for two major international brands:

- **HIMEL** — Low-voltage electrical protection and control products
- **Fuji Electric** — Industrial variable frequency drives (inverters)

### 2. Custom Fabrication & Engineering Services
Under the **Total Power Box** brand, T'sys designs and fabricates custom electrical assemblies:

- Low, Medium, and High Voltage Switchgear (LVSG / MVSG / HVSG)
- Panelboards and Distribution Boards
- Busduct / Busway Feeder Systems
- Cable Tray Systems
- Enclosed Circuit Breaker with Metering (ECBM)

Fabrication is performed by a team of certified electrical engineers using advanced **3D CAD/CAM mechatronics systems**, enabling full design-and-build capability comparable to foreign and global standards. Services include design, production, retrofit, and installation.

### Category — Variable Frequency Drives (Fuji Electric Brand)

Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs / inverters) for motor speed control and energy savings across industrial applications.

#### FRENIC-ACE — General-Purpose VFD
- **Application**: Most types of machinery — fans, pumps, compressors, specialized equipment
- **Capacity**: 0.1–590 kW (200V and 400V 3-phase; 200V single-phase)
- Sensorless dynamic torque vector control
- Customizable logic functions (up to 200 steps)
- Built-in RS485 / Modbus RTU (2-channel), Safe Torque Off (STO)
- 10-year lifetime design; removable keypad

#### FRENIC-HVAC — Slim-Type VFD for HVAC Energy Saving
- **Application**: Building HVAC systems — fans, air handling units, cooling towers
- **Capacity**: 0.75–710 kW (IP21 or IP55 up to 90 kW)
- First slim-type inverter specialized for HVAC energy savings
- Standard communications: BACnet MS/TP, Modbus RTU, Metasys N2
- Dedicated pump control function; SEMI F47 voltage sag immunity
- Suitable for integration into building automation systems (BAS)

#### FRENIC-AQUA — Slim-Type VFD for Water & Pump Applications
- **Application**: Water treatment plants, pumping stations, fluid control systems
- **Capacity**: 0.75–710 kW (IP21 or IP55 up to 90 kW)
- Optimized energy-saving control for pump and water system environments
- Same communications suite as FRENIC-HVAC (BACnet, Modbus, Metasys N2)
- Stand-alone operation capability; SEMI F47 certified

#### FRENIC-MEGA — High-Performance VFD for Heavy Industrial Loads
- **Application**: Heavy-duty industrial machinery requiring high dynamic torque
- **Ratings**: HD (Heavy Duty) and LD (Light Duty) versions; 200V / 400V 3-phase; single-phase 200V
- Same advanced logic customization as ACE (up to 200 steps)
- Sensorless dynamic torque vector control with optional PG feedback
- 10-year lifetime design; USB port option on removable keypad

### Category — Circuit Breaker (HIMEL Brand)

Low-voltage protective and switching devices used in motor control centers, distribution panels, and industrial control cabinets.

#### Miniature Circuit Breaker — HDB6s / HDB9 Series
- **Standard**: IEC 60898-1
- Protection against overload and short-circuit currents
- Available in 1P, 2P, 3P, and 4P configurations
- Current range: 1–63A
- Breaking capacity: up to 10 kAIC (HDB9H) or 6/4.5 kAIC (HDB6s)
- Trip curves B, C, and D
- Class 3 current energy-limiting; Quick Closed technology (20,000 mechanical / 10,000 electrical operations)
- Temperature rated: −30°C to +70°C

#### Manual Motor Starter + Contactor — HDP6 Series
- **Standard**: IEC 947-1, IEC 947-2, IEC 947-4-1
- Combines circuit breaker and thermal overload relay in a single unit
- Paired with **HDC6 AC Magnetic Contactors** (9–95A and 115–630A ranges) and **HDR6 Thermal Overload Relay**
- Designed for direct motor protection in low-frequency switching applications

#### Molded Case & Air Circuit Breakers — HDM6L MCCB / HDW6 ACB Series
- **Standard**: IEC 60947-2
- **MCCB (HDM6s)**: 16–800AT in 100AF / 250AF / 400AF / 630AF / 800AF frames; breaking capacity up to 50 kAIC @ 400V
- **ACB (HDW6)**: 630–6300AT in 2000AF / 3200AF / 6300AF frames; breaking capacity up to 120 kAIC; available fixed or drawout, manual or motorized
- Used for main incomer and bus coupler applications in large distribution panels
- Rated insulation voltage: AC 800V; impulse withstand: 8 kV
